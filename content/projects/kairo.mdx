**KAIRO** is a full-stack Customer Intelligence and after-sales management platform designed to centralize customer information, improve customer experience, and support data-driven business decisions.

The backend is built with **Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, and PostgreSQL**, providing REST APIs, authentication, role-based access control, and structured data management.

The frontend is developed with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS**, delivering a responsive, bilingual interface in English and Spanish.

**Key capabilities include:**
- Customer relationship and after-sales management.
- Customer satisfaction surveys and experience analysis.
- Customer segmentation and retention insights.
- Revenue recovery and business performance visibility.
- Authentication, role-based access control, and a public read-only demo.

The platform uses **Docker** for local development and is deployed through **Vercel** (frontend), **Render** (backend), and **Supabase** (PostgreSQL database).
